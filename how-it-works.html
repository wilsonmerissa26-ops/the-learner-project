How It Works

From the first session to every week after.

Every plan starts with understanding your child. Here’s what happens, step by step.

  1. 1

    Personal Learning Assessment

    Virtual or in person. One on one, I see how your child thinks, find where learning breaks down, and learn what they love. For reading and phonics, we start with a Reading Check instead.

    Starts at $199

  2. 2

    Your Child’s Learning Map

    A written map of their strengths, gaps, and plan, so you know what I’m teaching first and why.

  3. 3

    Lessons Built Around What They Love

    Short digital lessons and learning games I create for your child, with stories connected to their interests. You don’t need to teach the lessons. Your part is helping your child keep a routine and telling me when something isn’t working.

  4. 4

    A Weekly Update and a Monthly Check-In

    Each week I review their work and send you a written update: what they worked on, what clicked, and what’s next. Paid digital plans also include a 20-minute live check-in once a month, by video or phone.

  5. 5

    Teach and Check Again

    Later checks show what your child can still do on their own, and the plan changes as they grow.
