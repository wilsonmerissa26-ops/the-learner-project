Help

Need help? I’m here.

If a lesson won’t load, a login doesn’t work, or your child isn’t sure how something works, tell me. I reply as soon as I can, and always within 24 hours.

Try These First

A lesson won’t load

Refresh the page. If it still won’t load, try a different browser, turn off any pop-up blocker, and check your internet connection.

A login doesn’t work

Check for typing mistakes and try again. If it still doesn’t work, send me a message below and I’ll fix it.

My child is stuck or unsure what to do

Younger children need an adult nearby to help. If something is still confusing, tell me which lesson it was and what happened. That helps me fix it and teach it better.

Send Me a Message

Please use your child’s first name only. Don’t include diagnoses or school records.

When you submit a form, your answers go through a form service, Web3Forms, to my email. Web3Forms deletes its copy within 7 days. Read the privacy note.
