Questions

Does it match what my child learns in school?

Yes. I align lessons to your state’s standards for your child’s grade. For homework and class support, I follow your child’s actual syllabus.

How much time does it take each week?

Lessons are short, and learning games give extra practice on the skills in your child’s plan. You don’t need to teach the lessons. Your part is making sure they get done. Once I know your child, I’ll suggest a weekly rhythm.

Is this ABA therapy?

No. I use ABA teaching techniques to break skills into small steps, but this is academic learning support, not ABA therapy.
