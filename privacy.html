Privacy

What happens to the information you send.

When you submit a form, your answers go through a form service, Web3Forms, to my email so I can review fit and follow up. Web3Forms deletes its copy within 7 days.

Child information is used only for application review, planning next steps, and program communication. Your information is not sold.

Your answers to the home page questions stay in your browser during your visit, so they can fill in the application for you. They aren’t sent to me unless you submit a form.

If you request the free checklist, your first name, email, your child’s grade if you share it, and which link or site brought you here go through Web3Forms to my email. I’ll only send occasional emails if you check the box, and you can ask me to stop anytime.

To count visits, this site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, which doesn’t use cookies or collect personal information. Your browser also remembers if you closed the checklist offer, so it doesn’t keep appearing.

To have your details deleted, email me. A full consent and privacy agreement is completed before any child starts.

Questions? Email thelearnerproject2026@gmail.com.
