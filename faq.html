Getting Started

Who is it for?

Elementary, middle, and high school students who need extra support in reading, writing, vocabulary, spelling, math, science, or a mix of subjects. That includes children who struggle with reading, children falling behind in math, children who’ve tried everything, and children whose good grades hide a gap.

How do you figure out what my child needs?

The digital program starts with a Personal Learning Assessment, virtual or in person. I see how your child thinks, where they get stuck, and what kind of help moves them forward. You’ll get a written Learning Map with their strengths, gaps, and plan. Reading and phonics starts with a required Reading Check, which shows which sounds and letter patterns your child knows. Schoolwork you choose to share can help guide the plan too.

What happens after I apply?

Every family applies first, and I review each application myself. I’ll reply by email within 48 to 72 hours. If it looks like a good fit, we’ll set up a short call before anything starts. If it isn’t the right fit right now, you’re welcome to join the waitlist.

Lessons

Is every child doing the same lessons?

No. Each child gets a customized plan. Two children in the same grade may need different starting points, explanations, and practice.

Does it match what my child learns in school?

Yes. I align lessons to your state’s standards for your child’s grade. For homework and class support, I follow your child’s actual syllabus.

How much time does it take?

Lessons are short, and learning games give extra practice on the skills in your child’s plan. You don’t need to teach the lessons. Your part is making sure they get done. Once I know your child, I’ll suggest a weekly rhythm.

Is it all games?

No. Lessons and practice come first. Learning games are included when they practice the skill your child is working on.

What if we need tech help?

Tech support is included. If a lesson won’t load, a login doesn’t work, or your child isn’t sure how something works, email me and I’ll help.

Live Time and Updates

Are there live sessions?

The lessons aren’t live. Your child does them at home on a laptop or tablet with an internet connection. Every digital program includes a 20-minute live check-in with me each month, by video or phone, and more check-ins can be added. Reading and phonics sessions are live and virtual.

Will I know how my child is doing?

Yes. Every week, I review your child’s work and send you a written update: what they worked on, what clicked, and what’s next. Once a month, we have a 20-minute live check-in by video or phone. You can share concerns anytime.

Pricing and the Pilot

How much does it cost?

The digital program starts at $449 a month, and reading and phonics starts at $329 a month. The digital program begins with a Personal Learning Assessment, which starts at $199. Reading and phonics begins with a required Reading Check, which starts at $75. Your child’s price depends on their subjects and lesson days, and it’s confirmed in writing before you enroll. See Programs and Pricing.

Is there a contract?

No. Programs are month to month. You can cancel anytime with 7 days’ notice before your next billing date. Your program continues through the end of the month you’ve paid for, and there are no partial refunds.

How does the founding pilot work?

Two founding students get 30 days of the digital program at no cost, with weekly progress updates. Founding members pay $100 for the assessment instead of $199, and it’s refunded when you complete the 30 days. Before your child starts, we’ll sign a founding member agreement that lists what’s included. You’re free to stop at the end of the pilot, and nothing renews automatically.

What if the founding spots are filled?

Join the waitlist. When I consider future spots, I review the requests and may contact families whose needs fit. If I offer a spot, I’ll explain what’s included and any price before you decide. You can also email me with questions at thelearnerproject2026@gmail.com.

Support

My child has an IEP, ADHD, or dyslexia. Can they join?

Possibly. Tell me what helps your child learn and what support they already receive, and we’ll talk about fit. This is academic learning support. It doesn’t diagnose, and it doesn’t replace school services or therapy. For a child who is still learning to sound out words, live reading and phonics sessions may be the better fit.

Is this ABA therapy?

No. I use ABA teaching techniques to break skills into small steps, give the right amount of help, and step back as your child gets more confident. The Learner Project is academic learning support, not ABA therapy. It doesn’t replace school, therapy, or special education services.
